2. Cookies and tracking technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Pixels (also called tags or web beacons) are small pieces of code that tell a service a page was viewed or an action was taken. We use them to:

  • Make the Site work (for example, remembering form inputs or security checks). These are strictly necessary.
  • Measure and improve the Site (analytics).
  • Measure and improve our advertising, for example to see whether someone who clicked one of our ads went on to view a page or click an affiliate link, and to show our ads to people who have visited the Site before (remarketing).

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you do, some parts of the Site may not work as expected. [VERIFY: if you add a cookie-consent banner, add: “You can change your cookie choices at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer.”]

4. Affiliate links

Some links on the Site are affiliate links. When you click one, you go to the other company’s website, and that company (or its affiliate network, such as Impact, FirstPromoter or Rewardful) may place a cookie or record a referral ID so it can credit us with a commission if you sign up or buy. We don’t receive your payment details from these companies. At most, we may receive aggregated or anonymized reports of clicks and sales.

We may also record that an affiliate link was clicked (not who you are) through our analytics tools, to see which pages are helpful. Once you leave our Site, the other company’s privacy policy applies. Read our Affiliate Disclosure for more detail.
