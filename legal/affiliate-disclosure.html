In plain English: some links on GrowDigitalNY are affiliate links. If you click one and then sign up or buy, we may earn a commission. It doesn’t cost you anything extra. This is one of the ways we pay for running the site.

What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a special link that tells a company you came from our site. If you sign up for a trial, subscribe or make a purchase through it, the company may pay us a fee or a share of the sale. The price you pay is the same as if you went to the company’s website directly. Sometimes an affiliate link includes a discount or extended trial, and when it does, we say so.

Which links are affiliate links?

Most links to software products in our reviews, comparisons and guides are affiliate links. That includes buttons such as “Start your free trial” or “Try it free”. We mark them in two ways:

  • Every page that contains affiliate links shows a short disclosure notice near the top, before the first link.
  • Affiliate links are tagged in the page code as sponsored, as Google and Microsoft require.

We currently take part in affiliate programs run directly by software companies and through affiliate networks such as Impact, FirstPromoter and Rewardful.

Does this affect what we write?

We aim to be honest about every product, including its downsides. Here is how we work:

  • Commissions can affect which products we cover. We’re more likely to write about tools that have an affiliate program. We will still tell you when a tool isn’t a good fit, and we include pros and cons in every review.
  • Companies don’t pay for positive reviews. No company reviews or approves our content before we publish it, and an affiliate relationship doesn’t buy a better rating or ranking.
  • We’re clear about how we researched a product. Our reviews are based on the vendor’s own documentation, pricing pages and public information, and on hands-on use where we’ve had it. When we’ve used a tool ourselves, the review says so. When we haven’t, we don’t claim to have.
  • We check prices against the vendor’s site and show the date we checked. Prices change, so always confirm on the vendor’s website before you buy.

We sometimes run paid ads (for example, on Google, Microsoft Bing or social media) that send visitors to our review pages. Running those ads doesn’t change what the reviews say.
