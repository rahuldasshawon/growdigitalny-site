What is Systeme.io, in plain English?

Systeme.io is an all-in-one platform for selling online. You build sales funnels and landing pages, collect emails, run automated email sequences, host online courses, take payments and publish a simple website or blog, all from one dashboard.

Its pitch is simplicity and price. Instead of stitching together a page builder, an email tool, a course platform and a checkout, you get decent versions of all of them in one place. For someone just starting out, that matters more than having the fanciest editor.

The features that actually matter

Funnels and landing pages

Drag-and-drop pages for opt-ins, sales pages, checkouts and thank-you pages, linked into funnels with upsells.

Email marketing and automation

Newsletters, sequences and rule-based automations (tag, enroll, send). Every plan includes unlimited email sends; the free plan has 1 automation rule.

Courses and communities

Host video courses with drip release, and run a member community: 1 on Free, 5 on Startup, 20 on Webinar and unlimited on Unlimited.

Website and blog

Publish a basic site and blog on your own domain, so you might not need WordPress at all.

Evergreen webinars

Pre-recorded webinars with registration pages, starting on the Webinar plan (10 evergreen webinars).

Your own affiliate program

Let your customers promote your products for a commission, built into the platform on every plan, including Free.

Which plan should you pick?

If you're starting from zero, use the free plan until you hit a limit. That's the point where Systeme.io has proved it can make you money. Most solo creators can stay on Startup for a long time, so only move up for webinars or bigger lists.

Frequently asked questions

Is Systeme.io really free?

Yes. There's a free plan with no time limit, but it caps you at 2,000 contacts, 3 funnels and 1 course. It's enough to launch a first product.

Can Systeme.io replace ClickFunnels?

For most solo creators, yes. You get funnels, checkout, email and courses for much less. ClickFunnels has a more flexible editor and a bigger template ecosystem.

Systeme.io or GoHighLevel?

Systeme.io suits solo creators selling their own products. GoHighLevel suits agencies and service businesses that need a CRM, SMS and client accounts. See our GoHighLevel review.

Does Systeme.io take a cut of my sales?

No. Systeme.io lists 0% transaction fees on every plan. Your payment processor (Stripe or PayPal) still charges its usual fees.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, you can connect your own domain for your funnels, site and blog.
