What is Simplified?

Simplified is an all-in-one content app. You can generate copy from templates, design graphics from templates, edit short videos, and schedule posts to your social accounts from one dashboard.

The appeal is speed for small teams. The trade-off is depth: each part is simpler than a dedicated tool like a pro design app or an SEO writer.

For basic social graphics, often yes. Canva has a bigger template library and more design features, so heavy design users may prefer it.
