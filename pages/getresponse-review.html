What is GetResponse?

GetResponse is an email marketing platform that has grown into a wider marketing toolkit. At its core you build a list, send newsletters and set up automated email sequences. Around that it adds sign-up forms and pop-ups, landing pages, a simple website builder and AI help for writing emails.

What sets it apart for creators and small businesses is that the higher plans also include webinars and a course creator. You can grow your list, host a live session and sell a course without stitching together three or four separate tools.

Which plan should you choose?

If you're just starting a list, Starter is enough. Once you want automated welcome sequences and abandoned-cart emails, move to Marketer. That's where GetResponse earns its price. Choose Creator only if you'll actually run webinars or sell a course, because that's what the extra cost pays for.

Frequently asked questions

Does GetResponse have a free trial or free plan?

GetResponse offers a free trial so you can test it before paying. Whether there's also a permanent free plan, and what it includes, changes from time to time, so check the pricing page for the current offer.

Is GetResponse good for beginners?

Yes. The editor and templates are beginner-friendly, and the automation builder is visual. Advanced workflows take a little longer to learn.

Is GetResponse good for creators?

It's one of the few email tools with webinars and a course creator built in, so creators who teach or sell digital products get a lot in one subscription.

How does GetResponse pricing work?

You pick a plan, and the price is based on how many contacts are on your list. Bigger lists cost more. Paying annually is cheaper than paying monthly.

Can I move my list from another email tool?

Yes. You can import contacts from a CSV file or from other tools, as long as your contacts agreed to receive email from you. The FTC's CAN-SPAM compliance guide explains the basic rules for commercial email in the US.
