What is Journeybee, in plain English?

A PRM gives your partners one place to sign up, learn your product, register deals, grab marketing assets and track their commissions, while your team sees all of it in your CRM.

Journeybee focuses on keeping that in sync with HubSpot and Salesforce in both directions, so partner-sourced deals show up where your sales team already works. It also includes partner training (an LMS with certifications) and branded portals.

Frequently asked questions

What is a PRM?

Partner relationship management software helps companies that sell through partners (resellers, referral partners, agencies) recruit, train and manage them, and track the deals they bring in.

Is Journeybee an affiliate platform?

Not really. It's built for B2B partner programs such as resellers, referral and technology partners. Pure affiliate programs are usually better served by affiliate or partner-payout tools.

Does Journeybee integrate with HubSpot?

Yes. It has a HubSpot Marketplace app with two-way sync, and it also syncs with Salesforce.
