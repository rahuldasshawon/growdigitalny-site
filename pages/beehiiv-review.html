What is beehiiv, in plain English?

beehiiv is a newsletter platform. You write in a clean editor, send to your list, and every post also goes on a fast, SEO-friendly website that beehiiv hosts for you.

Where it pulls ahead is growth. A referral program rewards readers for sharing. Recommendations let newsletters promote each other. Boosts pay you for each new subscriber you send to another newsletter, or let you pay others to send subscribers to you. On the money side, there's an ad network and paid subscriptions.

The features that actually matter

Editor and website

A modern writing editor, and a hosted website with your archive, custom pages and your own domain.

Referral program

Reward readers with milestones (stickers, guides, calls) when they refer friends. Check beehiiv's pricing page for which plan includes it.

Recommendations and Boosts

Cross-promote with other newsletters through the recommendation network (included on Free), and earn or pay per new subscriber through Boosts.

Ad network

Get matched with sponsors and run ads in your newsletter without selling them yourself. Ad monetization starts on the Lite plan.

Paid subscriptions

Charge for premium posts. On paid plans beehiiv takes 0% of your subscription revenue (your payment processor still charges its fees).

Analytics and automations

Open, click and growth-source analytics, plus welcome sequences and automations.

Which plan should you pick?

Start on Free while you find your voice and your first readers. Move to Lite once you want paid subscriptions, ads or automations. That's where beehiiv pays for itself. Most people only need Pro to remove the branding or run webinars.

Frequently asked questions

Is beehiiv free?

Yes. The Free plan covers up to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited sends. Paid subscriptions, ad monetization and automations need a paid plan.

beehiiv or Substack?

Substack is simpler and has a built-in reader network, but it takes 10% of paid subscriptions. beehiiv takes 0% on paid plans. beehiiv gives you more growth tools, more control over your brand and site, and more ways to earn.

Can businesses use beehiiv, or is it just for creators?

Plenty of B2B companies use it for company newsletters. If you need sales automation like abandoned carts, a marketing-automation tool is a better fit.

Can I move my existing list to beehiiv?

Yes. You can import subscribers from other platforms, as long as they agreed to hear from you.

How does the beehiiv Ad Network pay?

beehiiv matches you with advertisers so you can run ads without selling them yourself. Ad monetization starts on the Lite plan. Check beehiiv's help center for the current payout model and eligibility rules.
