What is ContentBot?

ContentBot started as an AI blog writer and has grown into a content automation tool. You can generate outlines and full drafts, then build "AI Flows": reusable sequences such as "research topic → outline → draft → meta description".

With the importer you can queue many topics at once and let the tool work through them in the background.

Key features that actually matter

AI blog writer

Generate outlines and long-form drafts from a topic or keyword.

AI Flows

Build reusable multi-step workflows for tasks you repeat every week.

Bulk import

Queue a list of topics and process them in the background: up to 50 rows per import on Premium and 500 on Premium+.

AI chat

Ask questions and refine content in a chat view alongside your documents.

Frequently asked questions

Will Google penalize AI-written content?

Google says it rewards helpful, reliable content however it's produced, but mass-producing pages mainly to manipulate rankings breaks its spam policies. See Google's guidance on AI content.

What are AI Flows?

They're reusable workflows that chain several AI steps together, such as outline, draft and meta description, so you can run the same process on any topic.

Does ContentBot have a free plan?

No. The cheapest way in is a prepaid word pack at $0.50 per 1,000 words, and your first purchase includes 10,000 free words.

Can ContentBot write in other languages?

Yes. ContentBot says it supports 110+ languages.
