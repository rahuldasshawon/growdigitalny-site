Best for: Marketing teams that need brand voice

Jasper

Jasper is built for marketing teams. Brand voice, style guides and campaign workflows keep a team's content consistent. It's more than a solo writer needs, and priced accordingly.

Standout: Strong brand-voice and campaign tools. Watch out for: Pricier than most options here.

Best for: Sales and marketing workflows

Copy.ai

Copy.ai has shifted from a simple copy generator to a platform for automating sales and marketing workflows, such as prospect research and outreach drafts. Its pricing page now sells it as "GTM AI" for teams, starting with a 5-seat Chat plan.

Standout: Automates repeat go-to-market tasks. Watch out for: Less focused on simple one-off copy than it used to be.

Best for: Tight budgets and short copy

Rytr

Rytr is the budget pick: 40+ writing use cases for ads, emails and product descriptions at a low monthly price, with a free plan to start. It has recently added communication features too, such as connecting your inbox and daily briefings.

Standout: Low price and a free plan. Watch out for: Weak at long-form content.

Best for: Tracking your brand in AI search

Writesonic

Writesonic is now mainly an AI search visibility (GEO) platform. It tracks how ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews mention your brand, with content tools to fix the gaps.

Standout: Tracks how AI answers mention your brand. Watch out for: Expensive if you only want a writer.

Best for: Social media content

Simplified combines an AI writer, a design editor, video tools and a social scheduler, and now sells AI agents that run whole campaigns from a brief. It's great if social media is your main channel and you're doing it yourself.

Standout: Writing, design and scheduling together. Watch out for: Each part is simpler than a dedicated tool.

What about general AI assistants?

You don't always need a dedicated writing tool. General AI assistants can draft, edit and brainstorm too. They're less structured for marketing workflows, but more flexible.

Claude

Anthropic's assistant is strong at long-form drafting, editing and working with long documents you upload. There's a free plan, and Pro costs $20 a month ($17 a month billed yearly). Visit Claude (not an affiliate link).

ChatGPT

OpenAI's assistant is a flexible all-rounder for drafting, brainstorming and research, with a free plan and paid plans (Go, Plus and Pro). Visit ChatGPT (not an affiliate link).

PopAI

PopAI focuses on working with documents: chatting with PDFs, summarizing files and generating presentations. Read our PopAI review for details.

When to pick a dedicated tool

Choose a dedicated writer when you need templates, brand voice, team workflows, bulk generation or AI-search tracking. Those are the things general assistants don't do out of the box.

How to choose the right AI writing tool

  1. Start with the job. Short ads and emails, long blog posts, social content and brand-consistent team content each point to different tools.
  2. Check the limits, not just the price. Word, credit and seat limits matter more than the headline price.
  3. Test with a real task. Use the free plan or trial on something you actually need to write this week.
  4. Plan your editing. Every tool here needs a human edit for facts, accuracy and voice.

Our verdict

For most small businesses, start cheap: try Rytr or a general assistant's free tier for everyday copy. If social media is your main channel, Simplified saves the most time. Marketing teams that need consistent brand voice should look at Jasper or neuroflash. Agencies and SEO teams watching AI search should look at Writesonic.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI writing tool in 2027?

There's no single best tool. It depends on the job: Jasper suits marketing teams, Rytr suits tight budgets, Simplified suits social media, and AI-Writer suits research-heavy writing. Start from the comparison table above.

Is there a free AI writing tool?

Several tools on this list have free plans or trials, and general assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT have free tiers. Free plans usually limit how much you can generate.

Will Google penalize AI-written content?

Google says it rewards helpful, reliable content however it's produced. Mass-producing pages mainly to manipulate rankings breaks its spam policies. Read Google's guidance on AI content.

Do I still need to edit AI-written content?

Yes. Check facts, add your own experience and examples, and make sure it sounds like you. AI drafts are a starting point, not a finished product.
