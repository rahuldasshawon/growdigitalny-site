What is Rytr?

Rytr is an AI writing assistant that turns a short brief into a first draft. You pick a use case (for example, "Facebook ad", "product description" or "email"), set a tone and language, add a few details, and Rytr writes several options to choose from.

It's deliberately simple. There's no campaign planning or brand-management layer like the bigger platforms have. That keeps the price low and the learning curve short.

Rytr has also added communication features recently: you can connect your inbox, keep track of relationships and get a daily briefing. The writing tools are still there, but if writing is all you want, the Lite plan is the one to look at.

Key features that actually matter

Use-case templates

40+ writing use cases for common jobs such as ads, emails, bios and product descriptions, on every plan including Free.

Tone and language options

Pick a tone of voice and write in 30+ languages, which is handy for multilingual customers.

Built-in editor

Rewrite, expand, shorten or continue any paragraph without leaving the document.

Browser extension and briefings

A browser extension brings Rytr into other sites. Paid plans add connected sources and daily briefings, and Pro adds custom use cases.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rytr free?

Yes. Rytr's free plan includes 10,000 characters a month, so you can test it before you pay. The Lite plan ($9 a month) removes the character limit.

Is Rytr good for blog posts?

It can help with outlines, intros and sections, but it's strongest at short copy. For full articles, expect to do a lot of editing.

Is Rytr content original?

Rytr generates new text each time. You should still review and edit anything before you publish it, and run your own originality check on important pages.

Can Rytr write in other languages?

Yes. You can choose the output language for each piece.
