What is neuroflash?

neuroflash combines AI text generation, AI images and a brand assistant. The core is the Brand Hub, where you define your brand voice and upload company information so the AI writes like you rather than like a generic chatbot.

It's especially strong in German, which makes it a natural fit for DACH-region businesses, but it also writes in English and other languages.

Key features that actually matter

Brand Hub

Save your brand voice, style rules and company facts so every output stays consistent.

AI copywriting

Create ads, social posts, product copy and articles from templates or chat.

AI images

Generate images alongside your copy. Pro unlocks more image models than Essential, and both include royalty-free Unsplash images.

Privacy focus

Servers in Germany and GDPR-compliant processing, which matters for EU businesses. There's also a plagiarism check and a Chrome and Firefox extension.

Digital Twins: test ideas on an AI audience

neuroflash's newest feature is Digital Twins: AI personas that simulate a target audience. You pick an audience, ask it a question or upload a few versions of an ad, post or message, and get answers from up to 20 "respondents" at once, each with its own reasons and sentiment. The twins also rate every version from 1 to 10.

neuroflash says the twins are built from survey data: up to 255 data points per person from more than 1 million surveys collected since 2017. It pitches them for testing ad creatives and headlines, checking campaign messaging before launch, and quick audience research for product ideas.

Which plans include it: according to neuroflash's pricing FAQ, Essential gets basic access (1 target group with 6 twins), Pro gets 3 target groups with 10 twins each plus API and MCP access, and Enterprise is unlimited.

Our take: treat it as a quick gut check before you spend money on ads, not a replacement for real customer feedback. neuroflash advertises 85–98% accuracy but doesn't explain how it measured that, so test with real traffic before you commit budget.
