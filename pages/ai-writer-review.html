What is AI-Writer?

AI-Writer researches a topic, writes a draft and attaches citations to the sources behind its claims. You can review where each point came from, swap weak sources out and edit the draft.

Today it's built around research questions: you ask a question, and it answers from a corpus of more than 100 million open science papers, showing the exact paragraphs it used. Citations are the reason to choose it over other writers.
