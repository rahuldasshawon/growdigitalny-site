What is GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel is an all-in-one sales and marketing platform built for agencies. You get a CRM, a funnel and website builder, email and SMS marketing, appointment booking, a unified inbox, reputation management and automation workflows under one login.

The feature that sets it apart is sub-accounts: every client gets their own workspace. From the Unlimited plan you can white-label the desktop app with your own logo, and on the top plan ("SaaS mode") you can sell it as your own software and charge clients a monthly fee for it.

Key features that actually matter

CRM and pipelines

Track every lead from first click to closed deal, with drag-and-drop pipeline stages and full contact history.

Funnels and websites

Build landing pages, funnels and full sites with a drag-and-drop editor, so you don't need a separate page builder.

Email, SMS and unified inbox

Follow up automatically across email, SMS and social DMs, and reply to everything from one inbox.

Workflows and automation

Trigger follow-ups, reminders, tags and tasks automatically. This is where GHL saves the most hours.

Calendars and booking

Let leads book calls directly, with automatic confirmations and no-show reminders.

Reputation management

Send review requests automatically after a job and respond to Google reviews from the dashboard.

Which plan should you choose?

If you run a single business, start with Starter. If you manage marketing for more than three clients, Unlimited usually pays for itself quickly. Choose Agency Pro only if you're ready to package and sell GHL as your own software.

Frequently asked questions

Does GoHighLevel have a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with full access to that plan's features. You add a card at signup, and you're billed automatically when the trial ends unless you cancel.

Is GoHighLevel good for beginners?

It's powerful but not the simplest tool. Beginners can get going with the onboarding resources and pre-built templates ("snapshots"), but expect to spend a few days learning it.

Can I use GoHighLevel for just one business?

Yes. The Starter plan ($97 a month, up to 3 sub-accounts) works well for a single business that wants its CRM, booking, follow-up and reviews in one place.

What does "SaaS mode" mean?

On the Agency Pro plan you can rebrand GoHighLevel under your own name and charge clients a monthly subscription, turning it into your own software product.

Are there extra costs?

Yes. Usage-based services such as phone numbers, SMS messages, calls, email sending and premium AI features are billed on top of the plan. Optional add-ons cost extra too.
