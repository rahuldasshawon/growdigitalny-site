What is GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel is an all-in-one sales and marketing platform built for agencies. You get a CRM, a funnel and website builder, email and SMS marketing, appointment booking, a unified inbox, reputation management and automation workflows under one login.

The feature that sets it apart is sub-accounts: every client gets their own workspace, and on the top plan you can rebrand the whole platform as your own software and charge clients a monthly fee for it.

Which plan should you choose?

If you run a single business, start with Starter. If you manage marketing for three or more clients, Unlimited usually pays for itself quickly. Choose Agency Pro only if you're ready to package and sell GHL as your own software.

Frequently asked questions

Does GoHighLevel have a free trial?

Yes. GoHighLevel offers a free trial so you can set up your account and test the features before you're charged. Check the signup page for the current trial length.

Is GoHighLevel good for beginners?

It's powerful but not the simplest tool. Beginners can get going with the onboarding resources and pre-built templates ("snapshots"), but expect to spend a few days learning it.

Can I use GoHighLevel for just one business?

Yes. The Starter plan works well for a single business that wants its CRM, booking, follow-up and reviews in one place.

What does "SaaS mode" mean?

On the Agency Pro plan you can rebrand GoHighLevel under your own name and charge clients a monthly subscription, turning it into your own software product.

Are there extra costs?

Yes. Usage-based services such as SMS messages, phone calls and some email sending are billed on top of the plan.
