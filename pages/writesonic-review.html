What is Writesonic?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of getting your brand mentioned and cited when people ask AI assistants for recommendations. Writesonic tracks which prompts mention your brand, which competitors appear instead, and which sources the AI engines cite.

It still includes AI writing and SEO tools, but they now serve that goal: creating or improving the pages AI engines are likely to cite.

Key features that actually matter

AI visibility tracking

See how often your brand appears in answers from AI engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, tracked daily on every paid plan. Enterprise covers all 8 AI platforms Writesonic tracks.

Competitor and citation insights

Find out which competitors and source pages the AI engines mention instead of you.

Content and SEO tools

Draft and optimize articles aimed at the questions where you're missing.

Recommendations

Get suggested fixes, such as pages to create or update, based on the tracking data.

GEO means improving how often AI assistants mention and cite your brand when people ask questions related to what you sell. It's similar to SEO, but for AI-generated answers.

Yes, it still has AI writing tools, but its main focus is now tracking and improving AI search visibility.
