Should you buy an AI SDR at all?

An AI SDR multiplies whatever you already have. If your outbound works, it can scale it. If it doesn't, it scales the problem. You're probably ready if you can say yes to most of these:

  • You can describe your ideal customer precisely (industry, size, role, trigger).
  • You have messaging that already gets replies when a person sends it.
  • Your contact data is decent, or you have a budget for good data.
  • You have separate sending domains and warmed-up mailboxes, or you're willing to set them up.
  • Someone on your team will own the tool, review the output and handle replies quickly.

If you said no to two or more, start with an AI-assisted outbound tool, where AI helps a person write and send, before handing over the keys.

How to run a fair AI SDR pilot

  1. Pick one segment. Choose a single, well-defined audience you already understand, not your whole market.
  2. Set the baseline. Write down your current reply rate, meetings held and cost per meeting for that segment.
  3. Protect your main domain. Send only from separate, warmed-up domains and mailboxes.
  4. Keep a human in the loop. Approve messages for the first few weeks and check every reply quickly.
  5. Judge on outcomes. After an agreed period (commonly 30 to 60 days), compare meetings held, pipeline and cost per meeting against your baseline. Ignore "emails sent".

Compliance and brand risk

AI doesn't change the law. Cold email rules still depend on where your recipients are: CAN-SPAM in the US, GDPR and PECR in the EU and UK, and CASL in Canada, which generally requires consent. Some places may also require you to disclose when a message comes from an automated system, so check the rules for each market you sell into. Get legal advice before sending at scale.

Brand risk is just as real. An AI SDR emails in your company's name. One wrong fact, a mangled name or an awkward follow-up sent to a thousand prospects is a bad first impression at scale. Review samples regularly, and give the tool clear rules about what it must never say.

Our verdict

For most B2B teams, the best AI SDR setup in 2027 is a hybrid one. Let AI do research, first drafts and follow-ups, and keep people on strategy and conversations. If you have website traffic, try Qualified's Piper for inbound first. Frequently asked questions

What is an AI SDR?

An AI SDR (sales development representative) is software that does some or all of an SDR's job: finding prospects, researching them, writing outreach, following up and booking meetings. Some tools run outbound on their own, and others help human reps work faster.

Can an AI SDR replace human SDRs?

Usually not completely. AI is good at research, first drafts and follow-ups at volume. People are still better at strategy, tricky replies and real conversations. Most teams get the best results with a hybrid setup.

How much do AI SDR tools cost?

Pricing ranges widely, from per-seat plans to custom enterprise contracts, and many vendors don't publish prices. Get quotes, and compare the total cost (including mailboxes, data and seats) against the meetings you expect to book.

Is AI-written cold email legal?

The same cold email rules apply whether a person or an AI writes the email: CAN-SPAM in the US, GDPR and PECR in the EU and UK, and CASL in Canada. Some places also have rules about disclosing automated bots. See the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide, and get legal advice before sending at scale.

What should I measure in an AI SDR pilot?

Measure meetings held (not just booked), positive reply rate, pipeline created, cost per meeting, and any spam complaints or brand issues. Compare against your current process over the same period.
