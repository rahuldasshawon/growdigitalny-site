What is ParagraphAI?

ParagraphAI helps you write, reply to and rewrite everyday text. Type a rough idea and it drafts the message. Paste a message you received and it suggests a reply. Adjust tone, formality and length with a tap.

Its main difference from a general chatbot is convenience: it's built into your keyboard and browser rather than a separate tab.
