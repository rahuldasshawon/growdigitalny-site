What is Salesforge, in plain English?

Salesforge runs multichannel cold outreach. You import or build a prospect list, write sequences (with AI personalization per prospect), and send from many mailboxes at once so no single inbox looks spammy.

Around that core sit sister products. Warmforge warms up your mailboxes. Mailforge, Infraforge and Primeforge set up the sending mailboxes and domains. Primebox pulls replies into one inbox. Agent Frank, an AI sales development rep (SDR), can run whole campaigns and book meetings, priced separately.

The features that actually matter

Sequences with AI personalization

Multi-step email sequences where AI writes a tailored line or whole email per prospect from their data.

Unlimited mailboxes and warm-up

Connect many sending inboxes and warm them up with Warmforge. Unlimited premium warm-up is included on Pro and Growth.

Mailbox infrastructure

Buy ready-to-send mailboxes and domains through Mailforge, Infraforge or Primeforge. For example, 10 Mailforge mailboxes cost $30 a month.

Primebox unified inbox

Handle replies from every mailbox and your LinkedIn senders in one place (Pro includes 1 LinkedIn sender, Growth unlimited).

Agent Frank (AI SDR)

An AI agent that prospects, writes, follows up and books meetings. It's a separate product at $499 a month, billed quarterly or annually.

Multilingual outreach

Multi-language sequences are on the Growth plan, and Agent Frank works in 20+ languages.

Which plan should you pick?

Most teams should start on the entry plan with a handful of mailboxes on separate domains, warm them up properly and run one tightly targeted campaign. Only look at Agent Frank once you know which message gets replies. AI can't fix a bad offer.

Frequently asked questions

Is cold email legal?

In many places, yes, but the rules differ by country. The US has CAN-SPAM, the EU and UK have GDPR and PECR, and Canada has CASL, which generally requires consent. See the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide, and get legal advice before emailing at scale.

Will Salesforge get my emails into the inbox?

No tool can guarantee that. Salesforge helps with warm-up, mailbox rotation and infrastructure, but your list quality, targeting and copy matter most.

Do I need separate domains for cold email?

It's standard practice to send cold email from secondary domains, so your main domain's reputation stays safe. Salesforge's mailbox products are built around this.

Can Agent Frank replace a human SDR?

It can take over a lot of the repetitive work, but most teams still need a person to set the strategy, check the messaging and handle real conversations. See how it compares in our best AI SDR tools guide.

Does Salesforge work for non-English campaigns?

Yes. Multi-language sequences are on the Growth plan, and Agent Frank works in 20+ languages, which helps for international outbound.
