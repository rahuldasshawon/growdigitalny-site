How it works

  1. Discovery call. A video call to understand your product, audience, goals and timeline.
  2. Architecture and content plan. We map the site structure, key pages and the content each one needs.
  3. Design. You review designs for the key pages before we build the rest.
  4. Build and test. We build, then test across browsers, devices and page speed tools.
  5. Launch and handover. We launch, set up analytics and show your team how to manage the site.
