Frequently asked questions

How much should we spend on ads?

It depends on your market, competition and the value of a customer. We'll recommend a starting budget after the strategy call.

Which countries can you run campaigns in?

The major ad platforms run in most countries. Tell us your target markets and languages, and we'll confirm what we can cover in your quote.

Who owns the ad accounts and data?

We recommend setting up ad accounts in your company's name, so you keep all the data if you ever change agencies. We'll confirm the setup in your quote.

Do you guarantee a number of leads?

No. We don't make guarantees we can't control. We agree clear targets together and report results openly.

How do you handle privacy rules like GDPR?

We set up consent-aware tracking and forms, and we follow each platform's data policies. Your own privacy policy and consent banner need to cover the markets you target. We recommend legal review for EU and UK campaigns.
