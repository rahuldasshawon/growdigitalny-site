# Cookie consent banner (CMP) options: checked 2026-11-18

All facts below come from the vendors' own pricing pages and Google's own help page, read on 2026-11-18 by the cloud session.
Anything not confirmed there is marked [VERIFY].

## Why we need one
- Required before showing ads to visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and for AdSense there: Google requires a
  Google-certified CMP that uses the IAB TCF (Google AdSense Help, "Google consent management requirements for serving
  ads in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland", support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554116).
- Needed before adding Microsoft Clarity, the Microsoft Ads tag (UET) or Google Ads tags for EU/UK visitors (GDPR/PECR).
- Running ads as an advertiser needs Google Consent Mode v2; the IAB TCF matters once AdSense ads run on our pages.

## Google-certified list (support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554116)
CookieYes (CMP ID 401), Complianz (CMP ID 332) and Cookiebot (CMP ID 134) are all on Google's certified CMP list.

## Recommendation
Complianz Premium Personal ($59 for 1 year of updates) is the cheapest option here that includes both Google Consent
Mode v2 and the IAB TCF, with no pageview cap, and it runs inside WordPress. CookieYes Free is fine for Consent Mode only
(no TCF) while traffic stays under 5,000 pageviews/month; adding TCF later means CookieYes Pro at $25/month.
